## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Cardmaniacs marketing surfaces preserve an Apple-native editorial identity
The marketing website SHALL translate the Cardmaniacs app into a calm editorial web experience using asymmetrical composition, oversized rounded frames, layered cards, restrained liquid-glass treatments, and generous whitespace instead of default SaaS layout patterns.

#### Scenario: Primary section composition
- **WHEN** a visitor views a major marketing section on any top-level page
- **THEN** the section presents one dominant focal moment, uses left-aligned rhythm as the default anchor, and avoids equal-weight multi-column clutter

### Requirement: Typography follows an Apple-style hierarchy
The website SHALL use Apple-native typography with SF Pro as the primary family, SF Pro Rounded or SF Mono only as supporting accents, and a hierarchy that supports large editorial headings while keeping body copy highly legible.

#### Scenario: Editorial heading system
- **WHEN** the homepage hero or a page intro renders on desktop or tablet
- **THEN** the page shows a large editorial headline, a shorter supporting paragraph, and readable body sizing without introducing serif display typography as the primary brand voice

### Requirement: Composition follows a soft asymmetrical rhythm
The website SHALL use asymmetrical layouts, large quiet areas, and one dominant narrative axis per section rather than rigid equal grids or multiple competing focal moments.

#### Scenario: Section composition on desktop
- **WHEN** a marketing section is laid out on a wide viewport
- **THEN** the composition uses one focal cluster, keeps left alignment as the default rhythm anchor, and avoids presenting multiple equal-priority highlights at once

### Requirement: Shapes and controls echo the product chrome
The website SHALL use large radii, pill-shaped controls, sculpted frames, and layered shadows derived from the Cardmaniacs app chrome without turning the interface into decorative glass for its own sake.

#### Scenario: Button and surface styling
- **WHEN** interactive controls and framed content surfaces are rendered
- **THEN** they use rounded and pill-based shapes, visibly distinct surface layers, and restrained glass effects that support clarity rather than overpowering it

### Requirement: Product imagery is staged as editorial artifacts
The website SHALL present Cardmaniacs screenshots, cropped windows, toolbar fragments, and icon assets as layered editorial artifacts instead of relying on generic full-device mockups.

#### Scenario: Screenshot presentation
- **WHEN** product imagery appears in the hero or feature storytelling sections
- **THEN** the imagery is cropped, layered, overlapped sparingly, or framed as partial windows so the product feels curated and tactile rather than merely embedded

### Requirement: Reference inspirations are adapted, not mimicked
The website SHALL use Cardmaniacs-owned tokens, imagery, and product framing even when sections are influenced by Bear-like asymmetry, premium Apple-platform apps, or Apple developer marketing references.

#### Scenario: Using inspiration in a new section
- **WHEN** a new page section is created from external visual inspiration
- **THEN** the resulting composition uses Cardmaniacs screenshots, Cardmaniacs iconography, and site tokens instead of copying an external product’s layout, palette, or branding verbatim

### Requirement: Themes and surfaces remain calm and readable
The website SHALL support light, dark, and system-driven theme behavior while preserving clear content hierarchy, sufficient contrast, and decorative effects that never obscure copy.

#### Scenario: Theme switching
- **WHEN** a visitor changes between light, dark, or auto theme modes
- **THEN** text remains legible, cards remain visually separated from the page background, and decorative blurs or forms do not reduce content clarity

### Requirement: The visual system forbids generic SaaS anti-patterns
The website SHALL avoid generic startup gradients, dashboard-density grids, overbuilt glassmorphism, default corporate font stacks, center-aligned-everything page structure, and heavy device-mockup clutter.

#### Scenario: Visual QA review
- **WHEN** a page is reviewed against the design system
- **THEN** it does not rely on the forbidden SaaS and template-like aesthetics to communicate premium quality
