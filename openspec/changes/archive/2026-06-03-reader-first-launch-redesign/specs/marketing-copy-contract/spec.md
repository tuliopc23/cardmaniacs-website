## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Copy sells workflow with brand anchors

Marketing copy SHALL use “native Apple app” and “calm reading desk” anchors and describe Collect → Triage → Read → Highlight → Revisit—not generic productivity SaaS language.

#### Scenario: Sitewide pass

- **WHEN** M7 copy hardening completes
- **THEN** generic “all-in-one productivity” phrasing is removed

### Requirement: No unsupported feature claims

Copy SHALL NOT claim features absent from the product or listed as planned-only in the M0 guardrails document.

#### Scenario: Social sources

- **WHEN** Reddit/Mastodon/Bluesky/X are mentioned
- **THEN** wording reflects supported vs planned status accurately
