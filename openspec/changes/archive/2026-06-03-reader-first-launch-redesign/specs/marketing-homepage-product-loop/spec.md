## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Homepage follows the 12-section product loop

The homepage SHALL include: Hero, Problem, Core workflow, Feeds, Read Later/parsing, Highlights, Native control, Organization, Extensions, Platform craft, Pricing teaser, and Final CTA.

#### Scenario: Scroll narrative

- **WHEN** a visitor scrolls the homepage
- **THEN** the story progresses from positioning through workflow, depth features, platform craft, and conversion—without generic SaaS template rhythm

### Requirement: Highlights and parsing are first-class sections

The homepage SHALL dedicate visible sections to highlights (killer feature) and parsing differentiators (Wikipedia, GitHub README, PDF, EPUB, etc.).

#### Scenario: Differentiation scan

- **WHEN** a visitor scans mid-page sections
- **THEN** highlights and parsing are clearly surfaced with matching visual objects
